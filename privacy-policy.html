Privacy policy – Umpi

Last updated: 23.10.26

Umpi is published by Ping Pong Labs. The app is a scoreboard that works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

The app stores player names you enter, your language and match settings, the current match score and pause-game high scores on your device only, so a match can continue if the app is closed. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Privatlivspolitik

Umpi udgives af Ping Pong Labs. Appen indsamler, sender, sælger eller deler ingen persondata. Den har ingen brugerkonti, ingen statistik, ingen reklamer, ingen sporing og laver ingen netværksforbindelser.

Spillernavne, sprog- og kampindstillinger, den igangværende kamp og rekorder i pausespillet gemmes kun på din enhed, så en kamp kan fortsætte, hvis appen lukkes. Data forlader aldrig enheden og slettes, når appen afinstalleres.
